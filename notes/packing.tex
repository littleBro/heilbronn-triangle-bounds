\begin{abstract}
We replace one finite digit-position construction in OpenAI's Heilbronn
triangle manuscript by the classical ternary progression-free set.
For the manuscript's fixed dimension \(d=41\), the replacement has
\(k=3^{384}\) rather than \(k=T^2+1\).
Keeping the remaining parameter choices gives the conditional exponent
\[
 \eta_{\rm new}=\frac{2}{45435\cdot3^{384}+16}
       \approx 2.68582462243153\cdot10^{-188}.
\]
The universal packing interface and finite denominator comparisons are
checked in Lean 4.11.0. The transfer to triangle areas is a written
argument conditional on the stated upstream estimates; the complete
geometric theorem has not been independently formalized or verified here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Pinned input and the claim}
For a finite planar point set \(P\) with at least three points, write
\(\Delta(P)\) for the minimum area over all triples of distinct points.
The input is \emph{A power improvement in the Heilbronn triangle lower
bound}, dated September 25, 2026, from OpenAI math family 191
\cite{upstream}, pinned at commit
\[
\texttt{fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb}.
\]
The source and its PDF are preserved without edits. The manifest records
their SHA-256 hashes; the change is supplied as a separate source patch.
Put
\[
 d=41,\qquad M=\binom{4d-1}{d}=\binom{163}{41},\qquad T=\binom{M}{3}.
\]
The input uses \(k_{\rm old}=T^2+1\). Its final alteration argument gives
the paper exponent
\[
 \eta_{\rm old}=\frac{2}{45435k_{\rm old}+16}
       \approx3.17571403643291\cdot10^{-236}.
\]
Exact arithmetic gives \(2^{383}<T\le2^{384}\).
This note changes only the encoding of the \(T\) determinant summands.
The field dimension \(d\), polynomial decomposition, digit distribution
rule, and exponents in the other parameter choices are retained.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional transfer]\label{prop:transfer}
Assume the norm-polynomial decomposition, lattice estimates, orbit
estimates, auxiliary-cap estimates, and lifting/counting estimates in
the pinned manuscript, in their stated generality. With the replacement
proved below, there are constants \(c>0\) and an integer \(n_0\ge3\) such that
for every integer \(n\ge n_0\) there is a set of \(n\) points in the
unit square whose every triangle has area at least
\[
 c\,n^{-2+\eta_{\rm new}},
 \qquad \eta_{\rm new}=\frac{2}{45435\cdot3^{384}+16}.
\]
\end{proposition}
Sections 3 and 4 give the transfer argument and identify the precise
interfaces to those inputs. This is not a deduction from the original
headline theorem alone: the generality of its intermediate estimates
is essential.

\section{The finite packing}
For a positive integer \(T\), let \(m=\lceil\log_2 T\rceil\) and
\(k=3^m\). For \(0\le a<T\), write its binary expansion, padded to
length \(m\), as
\[
 a=\sum_{v=0}^{m-1}\epsilon_v(a)2^v,\qquad
 u_a=\sum_{v=0}^{m-1}\epsilon_v(a)3^v,\qquad
 \epsilon_v(a)\in\{0,1\}.
\]
The values \(u_a\) are an initial segment of the classical Stanley
sequence \(S(0)\); the underlying progression-free construction is
not new \cite{stanley}. Our application to the pinned manuscript
and its formal certificate are the subject of this note.

\begin{lemma}[Packing interface]\label{lem:packing}
Define positions in three separate rows by
\[
 i_a=u_a,\qquad j_a=u_a,\qquad \ell_a=k-1-2u_a.
\]
Each position lies in \(\{0,\ldots,k-1\}\), each row is injective
as a function of \(a\), and
\[
 i_a+j_b+\ell_c=k-1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a=b=c
 \qquad (0\le a,b,c<T).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The geometric sum gives \(0\le2u_a\le3^m-1\).
Uniqueness of ternary expansion makes \(a\mapsto u_a\) injective.
The range and the injectivity of all three rows follow.
The displayed equality is equivalent to \(u_a+u_b=2u_c\).
On the left, every ternary digit sum is at most two; on the right,
twice a digit is either zero or two. Thus neither side has carries,
and uniqueness of base-three expansion gives
\[
 \epsilon_v(a)+\epsilon_v(b)=2\epsilon_v(c)
 \quad\text{for each }v.
\]
For digits in \(\{0,1\}\), this forces all three digits to agree.
Hence \(a=b=c\). The reverse implication follows by substitution.
\end{proof}
Distinctness is required \emph{within} a row. Using the same positions
in the first and second rows does not identify their polynomial
coefficients or their random digit draws.

The Lean theorem \texttt{packing\_interface} proves this interface
for every natural \(T,m\) with \(T\le2^m\), using a binary decoder to
prove injectivity of the actual index-to-word producer. It does not
assume an unproved progression-free input. The theorem
\texttt{paper\_matching} specializes the result to the literal
value of \(T\) and \(m=384\). The equality of that literal with the
binomial expression is separately checked by two Python computations;
it is not a theorem in the current Lean file.

\section{Preserving the determinant obstruction}
The original norm decomposition, labelled
\texttt{norm:determinant-sum}, gives \(T\) triples of homogeneous
polynomials \(f_{1a},f_{2a},f_{3a}\) with
\[
 \mathcal N(X^{(1)},X^{(2)},X^{(3)})
   =\sum_{a=0}^{T-1}\det(f_{ia}(X^{(j)}))_{1\le i,j\le3}.
\]
We retain those polynomials and the label vectors \(X(\xi)\).
Their norm identity is the upstream input that makes \(\mathcal N\)
nonzero for three distinct labels.

Keep \(L=r^{10}\), choose a prime
\[
 100k^2L^3<B\le200k^2L^3,\qquad
 h=B^k,\qquad \tau=\left\lfloor B^{k-1}/2\right\rfloor,
\]
and use Lemma~\ref{lem:packing} for the designated positions.
At each position in each row prescribe the original polynomial residue;
at undesignated positions prescribe zero modulo \(r\).
Every digit is still drawn independently, conditional on its column
label, uniformly from \(\{1,\ldots,L\}\) in the prescribed residue class.
Each class has exactly \(L/r=r^9\) possible digits.
In particular, a residue zero digit is not fixed to the integer zero.
All digits are positive and less than \(B\), so each entry's lowest
digit is a unit modulo the prime \(B\).

Let the digit matrix of three columns give the integer polynomial
\[
 F(Y)=\det\left(\sum_{v=0}^{k-1}c_{iv}^{(j)}Y^v\right)_{i,j=1}^3
     =\sum_{s=0}^{3(k-1)}\gamma_sY^s.
\]
Expanding the coefficient \(\gamma_{k-1}\) by rows, all terms with
an undesignated position vanish modulo \(r\).
Lemma~\ref{lem:packing} leaves exactly the triples with the same
index \(a\). Therefore
\[
 \gamma_{k-1}\equiv
 \sum_a\det(f_{ia}(X^{(j)}))_{i,j=1}^3
 =\mathcal N(X^{(1)},X^{(2)},X^{(3)})\pmod r.
\]
This is the sole use of the detailed position layout in the upstream
determinant obstruction.

For any coefficient and any of the six determinant permutations,
choosing the first two positions determines the third. Consequently
\[
 |\gamma_s|\le A_0:=6k^2L^3.
\]
For \(S_C=\sum_{s=0}^{k-1}\gamma_sB^s\), the same carry estimates give
\[
 |S_C|\le\frac{3}{50}(h-1)<\frac h2,\qquad
 \left|\sum_{s=0}^{k-2}\gamma_sB^s\right|
     \le\frac{3}{50}(B^{k-1}-1).
\]
When the labels are distinct, \(\gamma_{k-1}\not\equiv0\pmod r\),
so \(|\gamma_{k-1}|\ge1\). It follows that
\[
 |S_C|\ge B^{k-1}-\frac{3}{50}(B^{k-1}-1)>\tau.
\]
Every other representative \(S_C+zh\), \(z\ne0\), has absolute
value greater than \(h/2>\tau\). This re-establishes the entire
upstream obstruction with the new positions, conditional only on
the retained norm identity. Multiplication by an element of
\(\operatorname{SL}_3(\Z/h\Z)\) preserves it.

\section{Downstream estimates and the new exponent}
The relevant upstream interfaces are as follows.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.34\linewidth}p{0.59\linewidth}}
\toprule
Input label & Why the replacement is admissible\\
\midrule
\texttt{orb:diagonal}, \texttt{orb:orbit}
& Apply to any fixed \(k\ge1\) and a matrix with a unit entry.\\
\texttt{orb:conditional}
& Explicitly permits arbitrary prescribed digit residues as functions
of the labels. It requires independent draws with \(L/r\) choices.\\
\texttt{aux:cap}, \texttt{aux:weights}
& Depend on \(h\), \(q\), and the independent auxiliary distribution,
not on the individual digit positions.\\
\texttt{sam:lifting}, \texttt{sam:bad-triple}
& Use the orbit law, uniform conditional moment, and the
distinct-label determinant obstruction.\\
\texttt{zero:weighted-zero}
& Applies to the stated row lattice and auxiliary weight; its
bounds use \(h,q,N\), not the original formula for the positions.\\
\texttt{alt:exponent}
& Uses any fixed admissible \(k\), with the retained scales below.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These are assertions about the hypotheses visible in the pinned
text, not an independent verification of each upstream proof.

The digit estimate retains
\[
 \theta=B(r/L)^4\le200k^2r^{-6}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the conditional expectation of the smaller Smith divisor is at
most two for all sufficiently large \(r\), uniformly over every
fixed triple of labels, including repetitions.
The collision probability is at most \(3r^{-41}\).
The new \(k\) is fixed independently of \(r\); changing its value
changes thresholds and constants, not these powers of \(r\).

Keep the independent auxiliary choices and box scale
\[
 h^{100}<q\le2h^{100},\qquad N=(hq)^{10}.
\]
With \(a_r=r\sqrt{N^3/\tau}\) and \(n_r=\lfloor a_r\rfloor\),
the upstream pair and bad-triple estimates give
\[
 \frac{\E Z}{n_r}
 \ll n_r(hq)^6N^{-3}
   + n_r^2(\log(2N))^2\frac{h}{N^3r^{41}}.
\]
The first term is at most \(r\tau^{-1/2}(hq)^{-9}=o(1)\).
For the second, \(h/\tau\le3B=O_k(r^{30})\) gives
\[
 O_k((\log r)^2r^{\,2+30-41})
       =O_k((\log r)^2r^{-9})=o(1).
\]
Deletion therefore leaves \(n_r\) distinct projected points with
every triangle area at least \(\tau/(16N^3)\), including exclusion
of determinant-zero triples. As in the input,
\[
 n_r^2\Delta(P_r)\ge r^2/64.
\]
The cardinality scale is obtained without rounding its exponent:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \beta&=15(101k)-\frac{k-1}{2}
         =\frac{3029k+1}{2},\\
 \alpha&=1+30\beta=45435k+16,\qquad
 n_r\asymp_k r^\alpha.
 \end{split}
\]
This gives \(\Delta(P_r)\ge c_*n_r^{-2+2/\alpha}\).
The prime-interval and subset argument in the input's final section
passes from these sizes to every sufficiently large \(n\): its
two-sided bound \(A_kr^\alpha\le n_r\le D_kr^\alpha\) is preserved,
and removing points cannot decrease the smallest triangle.
This proves Proposition~\ref{prop:transfer} under its stated inputs.

\section{Verification and limits}
The deterministic arithmetic certificate is
\texttt{certificates/ternary.json}. Its independent checker computes
\(\binom{163}{41}\) by an exact-division recurrence, computes
\(\binom{M}{3}\) using the expanded cubic, and determines \(m\) by
successive doubling. This differs from the generator's binomial and
bit-length operations.
The exact exponent ratio satisfies
\[
 8\cdot10^{47}<
 \frac{\eta_{\rm new}}{\eta_{\rm old}}
 =\frac{45435(T^2+1)+16}{45435\cdot3^{384}+16}
 <9\cdot10^{47}.
\]
This comparison and the capacity inequalities are also checked
in Lean on the supplied literal \(T\).

The verification command additionally checks the unchanged source
hashes, applicability of the source patch, 21 finite packing sizes
(195873 pair checks), 12 determinant-coefficient and carry examples,
five malformed layouts, and one corrupted arithmetic certificate.
These finite examples check implementation behavior; they are not
the proof of the universal packing lemma.
Lean reports only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound} for the general interface, and no axioms for
the finite numerical comparisons. No new axioms, admitted goals,
or native computation axioms are used.

The upstream comparator target uses the smaller exponent
\(1/(100000k_{\rm old})\) and an unbounded sequence of cardinalities.
This note compares the two \emph{paper} exponents
\(2/(45435k+16)\); it does not claim that modifying a definition
automatically transports the upstream Lean proof. The saved comparator
is a statement template, not evidence that its proof was rerun.

The full geometric theorem and its upstream estimates are not checked
by our Lean file. We have not constructed an explicit practical-size
point set witnessing this asymptotic exponent, obtained independent
mathematical review, or established priority for this application.
The general ternary progression-free method is classical, and no
optimality of the replacement is asserted.
